Ventricular septal defect

Ischemia: plaque and thrombus

An interactive 3D explanation of coronary artery disease: atherosclerotic plaque narrows the left anterior descending (LAD) coronary artery, and a thrombus can further obstruct blood flow. Increase the plaque, observe the effect on the heart muscle, and race the clock to save it.

Cherry eye

The gland of the third eyelid prolapses. In the Morgan pocket technique, the gland is repositioned and retained within a pocket created from the conjunctiva on the third eyelid. Preserving the gland helps maintain tear production; gland removal is not the goal.

Entropion correction

The lower eyelid rolls inward, allowing hairs to rub against the cornea. In the Hotz-Celsus technique, a crescent of skin is removed parallel to the eyelid margin to evert the lid: support with a lid plate, make the crescent incisions, remove the skin strip, and close with sutures.

Veterinary (dog) · 2D illustration

A hand-drawn, frame-by-frame 2D animation explaining sickle cell disease: inheritance of HbS alleles from carrier parents, the resulting hemoglobin change, HbS polymerization under low-oxygen conditions, red blood cell sickling, and vaso-occlusion.
