    Procedure images (5)

    Use the same camera position, zoom and lighting in every image, with the eye centred. Landscape 16:9 at 1920×1080 or larger works best. Images that are not 16:9 are cropped from the centre. Keep the instruments out of the frame, except the lid plate in images 2 to 4. Do not move the eyelid between images 2 and 4. You can also drag the image files onto this window, or copy an image and press Ctrl+V. On a phone, tap Upload and choose from Photos or Files.
