Medical review & sources
  • Aspirin: 162–325 mg, chewed, as early as possible. It stops platelets clumping; it does not open the artery. AAFP review of the 2013 ACCF/AHA STEMI guideline.
  • Which treatment: a stent is preferred if it can be done within 120 min of first medical contact; otherwise give a clot-buster within 30 min of arrival. Heart Foundation (Australia) 2025.
  • Clot-busters: restore normal flow in only about 50–60% of patients, need about 30–45 min, and cause brain bleeding in up to about 1%. Bangkok Med J, 2012.
  • How Fast muscle dies: in dogs, 38% of the at-risk muscle was dead at 40 min, 57% at 3 h, 71% at 6 h. Humans vary. Reimer et al., 1977.
  • Simulated: the blood-supply bars, heart rate and ECG are illustrations. For teaching only. Check the latest ACC/AHA guideline before clinical use.
