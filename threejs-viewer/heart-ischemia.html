    Medical review & sources
    • Aspirin: 162–325 mg, chewed, as early as possible. It stops platelets clumping; it does not open the artery. AAFP review of the 2013 ACCF/AHA STEMI guideline.
    • Which treatment: a stent (primary PCI) is preferred if it can be done within 120 min of first medical contact; otherwise give a clot-buster within 30 min of arrival. Heart Foundation (Australia) 2025 ACS guideline.
    • Clot-busters: restore normal flow (TIMI 3) in only about 50–60% of patients, take about 30–45 min to work, and cause brain bleeding in up to about 1%. Bangkok Medical Journal review, 2012.
    • How fast muscle dies: in dogs, necrosis starts about 20 min after the blockage and spreads from the inner wall outwards: 38% of the at-risk muscle was dead at 40 min, 57% at 3 h, 71% at 6 h. Humans vary with collateral flow. Reimer et al., Circulation 1977.
    • Simulated: the blood-supply meters, heart rate, blood pressure and ECG are illustrations, not patient data. For teaching only. Check the latest ACC/AHA guideline before clinical use.
